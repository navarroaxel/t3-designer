## What is established and what remains estimated

The footage confirms visible objects, relative arrangement, colors, material
families and opening types. The existing proportional plan remains the source of
room names, reported areas and provisional global topology. It explicitly marks
wall lengths and room shapes as estimated. These photographs do not establish
metric widths, heights, precise offsets, geographic orientation or wall thickness.

Use three separate claims in the app/data:

- **Observed:** an object or architectural feature appears in these sources.
- **Placed from plan + imagery:** its room and likely wall can be matched, but its
  coordinates are an estimate.
- **Estimated dimensions:** proportions and standard asset sizes support an
  editable first model; they are not measurements of this apartment.

Surface wear and damaged areas belong in the current-condition model. Exact
irregular outlines, staining and color matching remain approximate because of
wide-angle perspective, changing exposure and compression.
